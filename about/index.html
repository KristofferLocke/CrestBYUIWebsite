The work

DIFFERENT SKILLS.
ONE DIRECTION.

01 / MECHANICAL

DESIGN. BUILD. TEST.

Mechanical work connects design ideas with the demands of a working car. From component layouts and CAD models to testing and refinement, the focus is on how parts fit, move, and perform together.

02 / TECHNICAL

MAKE IDEAS REAL.

Technical work turns drawings and plans into physical systems. Fabrication, assembly, and careful inspection bring the build together, with attention to safe workshop practices, clear procedures, and the details that make parts work as intended.

03 / BUSINESS

PLAN. CONNECT. GROW.

Business work keeps the project organised and connected. Planning, documentation, outreach, and in-kind partnerships help coordinate the people and resources behind the build while sharing the team’s work with the wider community.

04 / ELECTRICAL

POWER THE PROJECT.

Electrical work connects power, wiring, sensing, and control. Understanding how low- and high-voltage systems interact helps guide thoughtful integration, troubleshooting, and testing, with careful attention to safety and communication across the team.

Our project

FROM CAD
TO COMPETITION.

We’re designing and building VIKING.1, an electric formula car, at BYU-Idaho. Students work on the chassis, suspension, electronics, and controls, then assemble and test what they’ve designed. We’re preparing the car for Formula Hybrid+Electric.

Student engineering, put to the test.

Founded in 2006 and run by Dartmouth’s Thayer School of Engineering, Formula Hybrid+Electric is part of the SAE Collegiate Design Series. University teams design and build formula-style hybrid or all-electric racecars, bringing engineering disciplines together with a focus on drivetrain innovation and energy efficiency.

  • ENGINEERING & PROJECT MANAGEMENT

    In the static events, students explain and defend their engineering decisions to expert judges. Project management judging examines how teams plan and carry out the work through a written plan, an interim report, and a final presentation.

  • PERFORMANCE ON TRACK

    The dynamic events test acceleration over 75 meters, handling and maneuverability on an autocross course, and durability over a 44-kilometer endurance run. Electric-only cars must complete endurance using the energy stored in their accumulators.
